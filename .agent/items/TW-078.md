why: `.agent/gap-report.md` G-1 and G-4, the two remaining states §24 had no card

**G-1, §2 branch 10 `due`** — a staged plan is running, the settle window has
passed, no reading logged. The card is §24.25: **"Time to test / You changed the
alkalinity dose four days ago. A reading today will show whether it worked."**
It shares §24.15's headline and has its own support sentence. §24.15 is a
**correction** and asks *where it got to*; this is a **dose change** and asks
*whether it worked* — `reef-chemistry.md` §1's distinction, running through even
here. The app today says *"Alkalinity needs a test to confirm the new dose"*
(`state.js:298`), which is a finding against §24.25.
no dose figure on §24.25, and §23.3 would permit one: the dose has recently
changed, which is §23.3's first case. §23.3 is a limit on where a figure may
appear, not an instruction to print one wherever it may. The card's subject is
the missing reading.
**G-4, the tested-but-inconclusive `settling` case** (`state.js:345-353`) — two
or more readings since the change, neither steady enough to call it settled nor
moving enough to want a change. The card is §24.26: **"Still too close to call /
Alkalinity is 8.6 dKH after two readings since your dose change. One more will
show which way it's going."**
what makes it its own card: it is the state where a reading set clears **none**
of `reef-chemistry.md` §30's three bars while a change is in play — not §30.3
(the readings are further apart than the noise floor, so "no response at all" is
not available), not §30.2 (not moving 2–3× the floor against the change), not
§30.1 (no third reading in one direction). **It cannot be built before TW-066**,
which implements the bars; without them there is nothing to fail.
three refusals, not interchangeable, and this is the thing to get right: §24.5 is
missing **readings** (no change in play); §24.21 is missing a **second reading**
after a change; §24.26 is missing **agreement** between the two it has. The gap
report found §24.5 and §24.21 being stretched over this third case.
§9.1 is unchanged by either: `due` and `worked` are unreachable by any existing
test and `due` has never been seen on screen by anyone. Wording a state does not
make it reachable — any new wizard test must supply a staged `activePlan`, and
that fixture work is shared with TW-077.
read with: TW-066 (§30's evidence bars — a hard dependency for §24.26), TW-026
(the contradiction states, which §24.26 sits between), TW-077 (the `activePlan`
fixture).
in plain terms: two situations where the honest answer is "I do not know yet".
You changed a dose four days ago and never tested it — the app now asks. You
tested twice and the two readings disagree — the app now says it is still too
close to call, instead of picking whichever guess looks better.
