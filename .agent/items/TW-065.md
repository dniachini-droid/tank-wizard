id: TW-065
title: Movement is magnitude or persistence — four threshold families become one rule
status: needs-approval
tags: —
order: 760

why: `reef-chemistry.md` §11's movement rule, added 2026-08-16 (Stage 5). Four
families graded movement on one element: `ALK_TREND`/`CA_TREND`/`MG_TREND` per
day, `DRIFT_GUIDE` per week, `RATE_RULES` per day *and* per week, and
`CONSISTENCY_RULES` as a spread. The gap report's own worked example: a 0.6
dKH/week drift is `hold` in the wizard (0.086/day, under `ALK_TREND.stable`
0.10) and amber on the history modal (over `DRIFT_GUIDE` 0.5 and
`RATE_RULES.weeklyGood` 0.5). **Both correct against their own constant.**
the rule to build: a level is moving if **either** the fitted daily rate exceeds
the element's threshold — 0.10 dKH/day alkalinity, `CA_TREND` 5 ppm/week,
`MG_TREND` 10 ppm/week — **or** the direction has held across the window and the
**total** movement over it clears §5's noise floor. Slope times window, not
slope alone. 0.02 dKH/day for three days is noise; the same rate for ten days is
0.2 dKH and it is real.
the quantity is per day, decided: it is how the tank is thought about and what
the engines already use. Weekly is the same figure multiplied. A spread over a
window is a different measurement and is not a rate.
what this retires: `DRIFT_GUIDE` (`src/lib/analytics/drift.js:17-21`) and
`RATE_RULES` (`src/lib/analytics/rate-analysis.js:15-18`) have no part in
grading movement. `CONSISTENCY_RULES` (`src/lib/analytics/time-in-range.js:75-94`)
survives **only** where `wizard-states.md` §22's steadiness verdicts need a
spread — that is a different question and its 18 figures are not touched by this
item.
what must not break: §11's original rule outranks both limbs — a level outside
its band and moving further out is never graded stable, whatever the rate.
`outOfBandWorsening` already implements it and takes position, trend and the
noise floor. Do not route it through the new rule in a way that lets a
below-threshold rate re-enter the hold branch; that defect is the one §11 exists
to prevent and it crashed a simulated tank to 6.87 dKH while the app said hold.
read alongside TW-064 — the second limb is the noise floor, so this item's
behaviour depends on which table is live. TW-064 first.
watch the blast radius: `state.js:308` (`steady`, the `worked` test) and
`state.js:464` (`moving`, the `recovering`/`worsening`/`off-target` split) both
read the `band` field these thresholds produce, so this item changes which of
§24.3, §24.17 and §24.18 fires. A parity run before and after is the minimum.
in plain terms: the app had four different rulers for "is my alkalinity
moving", and they disagreed with each other on the same screen. Now there is
one, and it counts slow movement that keeps going in the same direction as real
— which is exactly the case that used to be called steady while a tank drained.
spec: docs/spec/reef-chemistry.md §11 (the movement rule), §5, §4;
docs/spec/wizard-states.md §22 (what `CONSISTENCY_RULES` is still for), §13
repro: a series falling 0.02 dKH/day for ten days grades `stable` today and is
0.2 dKH of real movement — twice the noise floor.
tests it needs: the two limbs separately; the ten-day slow-decline case that
must now grade as moving; a test that no dose-affecting path reads
`DRIFT_GUIDE` or `RATE_RULES`; and the §11 out-of-band-worsening case, which
must be unchanged.
