why: `.agent/stage-6a-gaps.md` T-1, T-2, T-4, A-2, A-3, A-4 and A-5, decided by
the owner on 16 August and folded the same day. Six of them are places
`src/lib/classify/classify-reading.js` **refuses more often than the app it
replaces**, which was the intended behaviour under canon as it stood and is now
either confirmed or narrowed by decision. Each ruling is small; they are one item
because they are one function's behaviour and one test file's fixtures.
**1. A single reading states its position** (T-1, `wizard-states.md` §13's last
row). The band classifies from the last reading however thin the series; **only
the movement and steadiness axes refuse**, naming what they are waiting for —
*"2 more readings in the 14-day window — §30.1 needs three"* — plus when the next
test is due, per §23 example 5. `insufficient-data` is now reserved for a band
that genuinely cannot be found: no reading, no target range, or a configuration
§18 rejects. §24.5 is the card that governs and it states the position with the
note *"the position is known"*.
**2. "Missing volume" comes out of §13's last row** (A-4). Net volume is a dosing
input (`reef-chemistry.md` §17, §12); classifying a reading against a range does
not need it. The function never took a volume, so this changes no behaviour — it
stops the row describing a refusal that must not exist, which a checker written
against the row's text would go looking for.
**3. A value claimed by two boundary rules classifies at the alert tier, and
produces one notice** (T-2). Magnesium at exactly 1150 is the case: §10 floors
its alert-low there and a keeper may set 1150 as their range minimum. **Test the
alert tier first** — of two defensible answers the more serious cannot get a tank
killed. **One reading, one position, one verdict**: §20's one-live-notice-per-
parameter is the mechanism, and a surface rendering both an in-range line and an
alert line for one reading is a finding.
**4. `drifting` needs no proximity test** (A-5). In range and moving →
`drifting`, wherever it sits in the band; in range and not moving → `in-band`. A
level dead centre and creeping is `drifting`. **Do not wire §26's 12%-of-band
`nearEdge` into it** — that is re-authorising an existing figure for a new use,
which §27 rule 3 exists to stop — and do not mint a new one. Costs nothing on
screen: `drifting` produces no notice (§13).
**5. Total movement is last minus first** (T-4). §11's second limb and §30.1 set
the same bar; §11's explanation said *"slope times window"*, a third quantity
that parts company with this one on exactly the noisy series where the answer
matters. **§30.1 governs and §11's sentence is corrected.** §11's fitted rate is
untouched and still drives the first limb.
**6. §5's resolution floor governs movement only** (A-3). A floor is a rule about
differences, not levels. **A phosphate of 0.00 classifies `out-of-band-low`** and
`reef-chemistry.md` §29.4's phosphate-low warning fires on it — 0.00 is the
clearest case that warning exists for, and the strict reading would have silenced
it at exactly that reading once Stage 6e read its input from a refusal. The
function currently refuses the whole result with `below-resolution`; that goes.
**Nothing changes on the movement side** — no trend, verdict or dose change may
rest on a sub-floor difference.
**7. §18's alert offsets clamp to the range edge rather than refusing** (A-2).
Any alkalinity range wider than 2.0 dKH inverts against the ±1.0 default (100 ppm
for calcium, 400 ppm for magnesium), and §2 permits a range anywhere inside
7–11 dKH — so `range-configuration` refused a range canon allows. **Clamp the
derived alert to the range edge**, which is §10's magnesium floor generalised to
one rule. A genuinely broken configuration — no range, a range inverted in
itself, a minimum below §2's safe bound — still refuses. **Do not make the
offsets proportional to the range**; §27 rule 3's argument reads across.
consequence worth knowing on 7: a very wide range makes `out-of-band-low`
unreachable on that side and the level goes in-band → `alert-low` directly. That
is the honest reading of a range that wide and is quieter than refusing.
