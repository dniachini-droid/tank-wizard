why: `.agent/gap-report.md` G-21, *"the 16 August contradiction, written down and
shipped"*. `findings.js:466-557` renders, on the same screen as the wizard's
verdict: *"…The dosing protocol looks only as far back as your last dose change
and sees nothing to act on there, so no dose change is suggested yet — **but the
longer view is drifting.** Worth another test or two to see which holds."*
the decision, 16 August (Stage 5b): **`drifting` is a tile state and nothing
else.** It colours the tile and says where the reading sits. It produces **no
notice**, no summary line and no card — which is why §24's twenty-seven do not
contain one and why that is not a twenty-eighth gap.
**`heading-out-<param>` is deleted outright.** Not rewritten, not narrowed, not
given a threshold. Recorded in canon as a deletion with its reason so it is not
re-derived from the empty slot in the card set.
the reason, which is the part to carry into review: **it is a surface
reconciling two time windows in prose, and `wizard-states.md` §25.2 replaced
that with a structural fix.** The sentence holds the wizard's window and a
longer window side by side, notices they disagree, and explains the
disagreement. §25.2's answer was not to word it better — it was to decide what
each surface is for, so the two never make the same kind of claim and never need
reconciling. This finding is the reconciliation the fix removed the need for,
still running.
it is also D-9's fault one step further along: *"the dosing protocol looks only
as far back as your last dose change"* is a surface explaining the engine's
reasoning in order to excuse its own disagreement with it.
nothing replaces it, and the parameter is not left silent: the wizard says it
from `reef-chemistry.md` §11's movement rule over §4's window, in the card the
keeper opens, and §25.1's headline can name a **moving** parameter.
**do not confuse `drifting` with `moving`.** §25.1 slot 2 names parameters that
are moving — §11's word and §11's rule over the wizard's window — and §25.1 says
in terms it is *"not `drifting` or `unsettled`"*. A moving parameter may reach
the headline; a `drifting` band may not produce a notice. Deleting this finding
must not delete the headline's slot-2 clause, and the two must not be wired to
the same source.
`drifting` itself is untouched: it still classifies, still colours, still differs
from `in-band` on screen.
