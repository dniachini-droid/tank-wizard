why: `.agent/gap-report.md` G-35 and `wizard-states.md` §25.5, which deferred the
screen with *"it may not survive."* Decided 16 August (Stage 5c): **it survives.**
The inventory measured its size and not its contents, and the contents are not
what either the inventory or the conversation around it assumed.
**four of its nine analysis blocks exist nowhere else in the app**: coral demand
over time (consumption on a rolling window with an uncertainty band, per element,
plus whether demand is growing); skeleton laid down (alkalinity consumed
converted to grams of calcium carbonate a month, caveated for coralline and
abiotic precipitation); nutrient production (worked backwards from a logged water
change and the level's movement, with the honest note that a steady nitrate level
and a productive tank with matching export look identical); and dose-strength
calibration (solving solution strength from the keeper's own dose changes rather
than the bottle's label — a direct attack on the risk `reef-chemistry.md` §16
calls the largest in the app). **This is the analytical half of the app**, not a
second copy of the history modal.
and it already contains §25.2's structural fix, reached independently and before
this week's work — the demand block's own comment: *"Deliberately NOT a dosing
verdict. That call belongs to 'Should you adjust?', which uses a short window;
this is the long view … Both were previously giving verdicts on different
windows, which read as a contradiction."* The dose-strength block defers to the
wizard explicitly and says why. **A screen that already knows it must not form a
second opinion is not the screen to delete.**
**four sections come out now**, each against a decision already made — this is
the whole of what this item removes, and each goes on that decision's authority
rather than on a judgement about Insights:
  - **"Water change needed to hold a level"** — `reef-chemistry.md` §29.6, no
    suggested levers for nitrate or phosphate. This is a lever with a litre
    figure attached.
  - **The nutrient equilibrium projection** — `.agent/gap-report.md` D-6. A
    projection about an export regime the app cannot see.
  - **The target-range retarget offer** — §18's clause, withdrawn in favour of
    §28. The *"Your tank actually runs X–Y · Use this"* row goes with it.
  - **The N:P ratio block** — canon has no ratio reasoning, and Randy
    Holmes-Farley calls the approach a myth: aim nitrogen and phosphorus at their
    own levels, never at a ratio.
**the remaining five blocks are not respecified by this item and must not be
rewritten by it.** They stay as they are, bound by §19 like everything else:
whatever they show, they render the engine's verdict or stay quiet.
**specification is scheduled after 6f**, not deferred — the difference is what
let this screen sit unread long enough to be nearly deleted on a guess. It is the
last unspecified surface, and specifying it while the layer beneath it is being
replaced would mean writing against something about to change. The four removals
happen **now** rather than at specification time, because each is already decided
against and leaving them in means shipping four known contradictions for the
length of Stage 6.

repro: open Insights — the four blocks above render today, and the retarget row

tests it needs: none of the four blocks reachable on any Insights route; no
litre figure attached to a nitrate or phosphate suggestion anywhere; no
`suggestWorth`-style retarget offer surviving on this surface; no N:P ratio
computed or rendered; and the four surviving analysis blocks still rendering,
asserted so the removal cannot quietly take them with it.
