Ammonia has been in `PARAM_DEFS` since before canon existed, with its own
confirmation branch (`ReadingConfirmation.jsx:343-353`), two findings
(`findings.js:213-236`) and a `CONSISTENCY_RULES` entry it can never use — and
**no section anywhere**. `reef-chemistry.md` §25's coverage table did not list
it at all. §32 is now that section.
the rule, decided 16 August (Stage 5b): **two states and nothing else.** A
reading of **zero is undetectable** and the app says **nothing at all**; a
reading **above zero is detectable** and produces **one notice, at alert tier,
on that single reading**.
`wizard-states.md` §13 does **not** classify ammonia. Its seven bands assume a
target range with two edges, a width and a midpoint; ammonia's target is zero.
Do not map undetectable to `in-band` or detectable to `alert-high` in the band
layer — §13 is amended to say ammonia sits outside it, and §18's alert row is
what places the tier.
silence is the decision, not an omission: no verdict, no notice, no tile state,
no tank-summary entry, **no clause in §25.1's collapsed headline**. The reading
is still stored, charted and shown as a number on its tile. §32.3 carries the
reasoning — a parameter that says "fine" every time is a line the keeper stops
reading, and the cost falls due exactly once.
**this is the only parameter with no quiet verdict.** §24.1's shape has no
ammonia equivalent and must not be written for one. §20's one-live-notice-per-
parameter is a ceiling, not a quota.
one reading is enough. **No evidence bar.** `reef-chemistry.md` §30's three bars
are about *movement* and none applies; a rule requiring a second detectable
reading before speaking would be an evidence bar on a fact. This is the only
place in the app that acts on a single reading.
what must be unreachable for ammonia, from every surface: **a trend** (§5 has no
floor for it and will not gain one — 0.5 then 0.25 is not a fall the app
reports), **a steadiness verdict** (§22's six do not apply; the
`CONSISTENCY_RULES` entry is deleted, not left as dead config), **a dose** (§2's
ordered list gains no row; §28's return plans do not reach it), and **an
analysis window** (§4 sets none; each reading is judged alone).
the two findings collapse to one. `ammonia-high` and `ammonia-detected` are two
tiers over one state, and a second tier is a band. 0.25 ppm and 2.0 ppm are the
same verdict.
wording is **not** drafted in canon and is part of this item — drafting work
constrained by a decided rule, the same standing as §25.2's `sliding` headline.
Bound by §14, §23.5 (no causes — a cycle, a dead animal, a filter crash, an
overfeed and a dosing accident are all consistent with the same reading) and
§29.6 read across (no levers). **Naming the target, zero, is not a lever.**
the off switch reaches it and that is intended (§32.6, `wizard-states.md`
§25.1). Do not special-case ammonia out of TW-082.
**Stage 6a's `classifyReading` landed before §32 was written, and half of it
already agrees — 16 August, after the merge.** `src/lib/classify/classify-reading.js`
reads **detectable as a recorded value greater than zero** (`:502-506`), which is
exactly §32.2's definition, and its gap **A-1** in `.agent/stage-6a-gaps.md` asked
for that to be confirmed. **§32 confirms it**, and A-1 can be closed against this
section rather than carried. **The owner confirmed it directly on 16 August and
`reef-chemistry.md` §18's row now says so too** (`docs/spec/stage-6a-decisions.md`
§3): detectable is any reading above zero, and the consequence is accepted with
it — **0.01 reaches the same tier as 0.5 ppm**, because the tier is about presence
and not amount and ammonia has no second tier to escalate into. **A-1 is closed,
not carried.**
**and ammonia is confirmed out of three more tables the same day**, so the
absences are decisions rather than holes: no analysis window (`reef-chemistry.md`
§4), no rate threshold and no `sliding` bar (§11), no clearly-out margin (§27),
and **no steadiness verdict** (`wizard-states.md` §22's figures — ammonia is one
of the five parameters with none). §5's floor was narrowed to govern movement
only, and §5 carries a sentence stating that this does **not** reach ammonia's
detectability rule in either direction. Nothing here changes what this item
builds; it removes four places a later reader could have filed ammonia a figure.
**Where it does not yet agree:** `classifyBand` returns one of §13's seven bands
for **every** ammonia reading, so a zero reading classifies `in-band`. **§32.1
says §13 does not classify ammonia at all** — two states, and `in-band` would
have to mean exactly zero, which is a value. Ammonia must leave the band path and
take the two-state path. This is not a defect Stage 6a introduced; the function
was built from canon as it stood, and §32 is newer than it. **6f's switchover is
where it bites** — until then nothing calls the function.
also already right for the wrong reason: ammonia has no analysis window, so the
movement and steadiness paths refuse for it already (`.agent/stage-6a-gaps.md`,
salinity/potassium/pH/ammonia). **§32.5 makes that a rule instead of a
consequence of a missing figure**, which is the difference between refusing and
never being asked.
