title: Brand picker in Setup — choose a product and it populates the solution strengths
status: needs-approval
tags: —
order: 550

why: the 16 August decision removed the shipped strength defaults, because a
figure the user has not checked against their own bottle is worse than a blank
field. That is correct and it leaves the user with an empty box and a unit
label (`dKH/mL/100L`) that most keepers have never had to think in. The
long-term answer is not to guess for them and not to leave them stranded, but
to attach the figure to something they can actually check: a named product.
Pick "Aquaforest Balling, 2x standard" and the app fills in the strengths —
and because the entry is named, the user can hold it against the bottle in
their hand and see whether it is theirs. That is the property a bare default
never had: 0.0533 is uncheckable, "Aquaforest Balling 2x = 0.0533" is not.
in plain terms: instead of asking you for a number nobody outside a chemistry
lab thinks in, ask which product you use and fill the number in from that.
You can see the name, so you can tell whether it is your bottle. A number on
its own you can only accept or ignore.
what it needs: a small product table (name, element, strength, and the source
the figure came from), a picker in the Setup strength field, and a free-text
"my own mix" path that leaves the field empty exactly as now. Selecting a
product writes the strength AND the product name, so a later reader can see
which bottle the figure claims to describe — which also gives TW-054 its
provenance marker for free.
constraints: the picker must not become a new way to ship an unverified
number. A product entry needs its figure derived and recorded (grams per
litre, salt, purity), not copied off a forum, and the app should show that
derivation next to the choice. A product whose figure cannot be sourced does
not go in the table. Dilution is the user's: every entry must state the mix it
assumes ("2x standard"), and picking one must not hide that they may have
mixed it differently.
spec: docs/spec/reef-chemistry.md §16, "Solution strengths are not shipped at
all", which names this as the better long-term answer. Note the picker does
not weaken that section: the refusal still applies until a product is chosen
or a figure typed.
