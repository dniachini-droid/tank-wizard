title: The three evidence bars — movement from nothing, a change contradicted, no response at all
status: needs-approval
tags: —

why: `reef-chemistry.md` §30, added 2026-08-16 (Stage 5), which the gap report
called the one thing to answer if only one thing got answered. Four registered
cards cannot be built without these bars — `wizard-states.md` §24.7, §24.8,
§24.21 and §24.22 — and they are the four cells `doseStatus` cannot express
(TW-026).
the three bars, and both figures in each are derived from §5 and §4 rather than
minted:
  - **movement from nothing** — three readings, one direction, total movement
    clearing §5's noise floor. One is notice, two is a signal, three is a fact.
  - **a dose change contradicted** — two readings, both dated after the change,
    at least **one test cadence** apart, moving **two to three times** the
    element's noise floor **opposite** to what the change intended. Alkalinity
    0.2-0.3 dKH at 2 days, calcium 20-30 ppm at 7 days, magnesium 60-90 ppm at
    21 days.
  - **no response at all** — two readings after the change, both within the
    noise floor of each other. An expected response that does not arrive is
    evidence, and the app has never been able to say it.
what changed from the draft this item was first filed against: the movement
figure is **proportional to each element's noise floor**, not a fixed 0.2-0.3
dKH — that was alkalinity's case mistaken for the rule. The interval is **one
test cadence, not 24 hours**; two magnesium readings are three weeks apart by
definition, so a 24-hour rule never constrained it. Both withdrawals are
recorded at §30.2.
the middle bar is deliberately the lowest and must not be "tidied" upward: a
dose change creates an expectation, so breaking it is informative at once, where
a trend from nothing has to earn its third reading.
none of this exists today. `doseStatus` reads `a.band` and `a.trendPerDay` with
**no reading count and no minimum spacing anywhere in the path**. TW-007 is the
same absence one layer down (`assessDrift`/`assessAlkalinity` producing
dose-affecting verdicts with no minimum-evidence gate) — the two should be read
together and may share one fix.
two things §30 leaves open and an implementer may not settle by choosing: how
30.1 relates to `directional()`'s four-row statistical gate where both could
apply, and whether claiming a change **worked** uses the contradiction bar or
stays a stability question under §11 (§24.6 depends on the answer). Both are in
`.agent/needs-dan.md`.
in plain terms: three different things the app can tell you about your tank, and
how sure it has to be before it says each one. Three tests going the same way to
call something a trend. Two tests since a dose change, a normal testing interval
apart, moving the wrong way by two or three times what the kit can resolve, to
say your change is being contradicted. And two tests that have not moved at all
to say nothing happened — which is the one it has never been able to say.
