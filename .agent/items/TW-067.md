id: TW-067
title: Alert levels for ammonia and salinity; potassium and pH deliberately have none
status: needs-approval
tags: —
order: 780

why: `reef-chemistry.md` §18, amended 2026-08-16 (Stage 5), and
`wizard-states.md` §13. Only alkalinity, calcium and magnesium had a *needs
attention* tier, so `positionBand` (`src/lib/analytics/reading-meaning.js:104-116`)
returned tier 1 for the other six and **could never return tier 2 however far
out they went** — seven bands on paper, five in practice, and no document
admitted it. The code comment at `:94-97` says as much.
what to build:
  - **Ammonia — anything detectable**, high side only. The target is zero, so
    there is no band edge to hang a margin from; the tier fires above whatever
    the kit resolves. `findings.js:229` already has a detectability test
    (`> step/2`) — settle whether that is the same threshold or a second one
    before writing a third.
  - **Salinity — below 33 ppt or above 36.5 ppt.** Fixed levels, not offsets from
    a midpoint, and inside `SAFE_BOUNDS`' 32-37 by design: outside where anyone
    runs, not yet at harm. **The high level was 36 and moved to 36.5 on
    16 August (Stage 6a, TW-086)** — 36 was the shipped range's own upper edge, so
    36.0 classified `alert-high` **and** not out of range at the same instant,
    which is a state no §24 card covers. **Build 36.5, not 36**; the ammonia
    detectability question above is separately confirmed at `reef-chemistry.md`
    §18 and §32.2 as **any reading above zero**, so `findings.js:229`'s `> step/2`
    is a second threshold and goes.
  - **Potassium and pH — no alert tier**, and this must be expressed as a
    decision in the code rather than as an absent table row, or the next reader
    files it as the same gap again.
  - **Phosphate and nitrate are already answered** by §29.4's two fixed
    warnings and get no alert pair here.
the structural difference to get right: the three existing rows are offsets from
the band midpoint (`ALERT_WIDTH`, `:102`) and the two new ones are absolute
figures. `positionBand` takes a midpoint and a width today, so it needs a second
shape rather than a fabricated width — deriving ±widths that happen to land on
33 and 36 would tie salinity's alert to whatever range the user sets, which is
not what §18 says.
do not disturb: the magnesium gate reads `magnesiumAlertLow`
(`src/lib/dosing/magnesium-gate.js:60`) off this same machinery and Stage 2a
just built it. §10's alert-low floor at `SAFE_BOUNDS.magnesium.min` stays.
N-2, still open and not settled by this item: `settings.mgAlertLow`
(`magnesium-gate.js:55`) is a live per-user override with no Setup field. §18
says alert thresholds are `[user]` adjustable; `wizard-states.md` §21 names
notification thresholds as a judgement Setup may not ask for. The app has half
of each. Carried in `.agent/needs-dan.md`.
in plain terms: six of your nine parameters could never show "needs attention",
no matter how far out they were — ammonia included, which is the one that
actually kills things. Ammonia now flags at anything the kit can see, and
salinity outside 33 to 36. Potassium and pH deliberately do not get an alarm
level, because they are watch-it parameters rather than act-now ones.
spec: docs/spec/reef-chemistry.md §18 (the added rows and the reasoning), §10,
§29.4; docs/spec/wizard-states.md §13, §22's alert tier
repro: `positionBand` with a salinity of 40 returns tier 1 — no alert — on a
reading that would be visibly wrong to any reefkeeper.
tests it needs: a boundary table per new threshold, inclusive edges per §13; a
test that potassium and pH never return an alert band; and the ammonia case at
exactly the kit's resolution.
