status: needs-approval

why: `.agent/gap-report.md` §25.6 item 2, decided 16 August (Stage 5c). Two
registered cards claim one reading below the safe floor — `wizard-states.md`
§24.9 (*"Alkalinity is very low at 6.8 dKH — this needs a correction unless you
are deliberately holding it there"*) and §24.23 (*"…at 6.9 dKH — bringing it to
8.5 would take about nine days at a safe rate"*, plus the return-plan offer). At
6.9 dKH the level is below `SAFE_BOUNDS`, so both apply to the same tank at the
same instant.
the decision is **placement, not a merge and not a deletion**: **§24.9 renders on
the dashboard, §24.23 renders in the wizard.** The dashboard wants a glance —
the level, and that it needs a correction. §24.23's duration and its offer only
matter once the keeper is somewhere a plan can be set. **Neither card is
reworded.** Neither is deleted.
**a third claimant was already gone** and is not part of this: `far-out-<param>`
(`src/lib/findings.js:243-277`) carries its own "dangerously low" title, and
*dangerously* is banned by §15 — it goes with `.agent/gap-report.md` D-4.
**this deletes `src/lib/narrative-engine.js:457-459`**, which is the point of the
item. Those lines are a hand-written regex over the wizard's own English —
`/level is not|steady but/i`, guarded by the presence of a `far-out-<param>`
finding and a `off-target`/`suggested` state — written for no reason other than
to stop these two claims colliding on one screen. **With a placement rule they
cannot collide**, so it has nothing left to suppress. It must not be replaced
with a narrower regex, a state check, or a priority field: **a surface deciding
what to show by pattern-matching another surface's prose is the fault
`wizard-states.md` §11's single-source rule exists to prevent**, and it is the
same shape as the `heading-out-*` deletion in TW-080.
what is **not** closed by this: whether §24.23's *"Plan a gradual return"* is a
return plan or a correction wearing the registered phrase (`reef-chemistry.md`
§28.2 offers a return plan only for a level **stable and out of band**, and a
level this far down is usually neither). That shared an open item with the
overlap and did not close with it — `wizard-states.md` §25.6 item 7,
`.agent/needs-dan.md` item 15. **Stage 6c renders this card and an implementer
may not pick the phrase.**
