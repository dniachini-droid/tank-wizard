id: TW-054
title: Phosphate and nitrate bands — ratify the defaults and honour the wide legitimate range
status: needs-approval
tags: —
order: 680

why: `reef-chemistry.md` §29.2, decided 16 Aug. The suggested bands are
phosphate 0.03-0.10 ppm and nitrate 5-15 ppm — which is what `PARAM_DEFS`
already carries (`src/lib/constants.js:41-42`) — and the safe bounds are
phosphate 0.01-0.5 and nitrate 0.5-50, which is what `SAFE_BOUNDS` already
carries (`src/lib/findings.js:125-126`). Nothing in those four figures moves.
**What is not proved is the second half of the decision**: the range of
legitimate settings is wider than for the dosed elements, and *"a user
targeting 0.40 ppm phosphate or 40 ppm nitrate is not making a mistake, and the
app may not treat them as one, nudge them toward the suggestion, or grade them
against it."* That is a claim about Setup, band validation and every surface
that compares a reading to layer 3 rather than to the user's own band, and
today it is asserted nowhere.
in plain terms: the app already starts you at the right ranges for phosphate
and nitrate. What nobody has checked is whether it stays quiet when you change
them to something perfectly reasonable but far from its suggestion — running
phosphate at 0.40, say, which plenty of people do.
scope, precisely:
  - pin the four figures as canon-conformance assertions, so a later edit to
    `PARAM_DEFS` or `SAFE_BOUNDS` that contradicts §29.2 fails a test rather
    than shipping. §16's universal-constants table is the model.
  - prove a phosphate target of 0.40 and a nitrate target of 40 are accepted
    by Setup and by band validation (§18) without a warning, a refusal or a
    correction toward the suggestion.
  - prove layer 3 is never re-applied and never used as a yardstick: no
    surface may grade a reading against 0.03-0.10 or 5-15 when the user has
    set something else. §2 layer 3 already says "editable, never re-applied";
    this is that rule tested on the two parameters whose spread is widest.
  - **out of scope: changing any of the four figures.** §29.2 promotes them
    from code comment to canon precisely so they stop moving.
spec: docs/spec/reef-chemistry.md §29.2, §29.1; §2's three layers; §18 for
band validation
repro: none written. The first artefact is a test that sets a phosphate target
of 0.40 and asserts the app says nothing about it.
owner: implementer, once approved
