why: `THE-ENGINE-PLAN-v2.md` Stage 6a. `.agent/engine-inventory.md` counted
**sixteen position classifiers** across ~76 decision sites with no referee, and
that structure is what let a card saying "alkalinity is rising" sit above a
panel reading −0.50 dKH/wk. This replaces all sixteen with one function; the
switchover that deletes them is 6f, not this item.
what it is: `src/lib/classify/classify-reading.js`, one exported function
answering four questions about one reading and nothing else —
  - its `wizard-states.md` §13 band, from canon's seven and no others;
  - its §22 steadiness verdict over the analysis window;
  - whether it is moving, per `reef-chemistry.md` §11 as amended 16 August —
    the fitted daily rate exceeds the element's threshold, **or** the direction
    has held and the total movement over the window clears §5's noise floor;
  - whether it is out and whether clearly out, per §26 and §27, from the last
    reading only and never a fitted value.
every threshold is Stage 5's: one noise-floor table (§5, eight parameters, all
absolute, ammonia deliberately absent), alert levels for ammonia at anything
detectable and salinity below 33 / above 36 (§18), the clearly-out margins of
§27 and §29.3, and §18's midpoint-hung offsets with §10's magnesium floor.
§18's own sentence naming this function — "classifyReading validates this on
every call and returns insufficient-data with a configuration error" — is
implemented and tested.
built from canon alone, per the plan's governing rule: nothing was read out of
`findings.js`, `narrative-engine.js`, `reading-meaning.js`, `stability-engine.js`,
`state.js` or the three engines. Where canon is silent the function **refuses and
names what is missing** rather than inheriting a figure — §13's last row and
§22's "Unknown refuses" are the mechanism canon already provides.
the gaps that produced: **twenty**, written up in `.agent/stage-6a-gaps.md`
with options, costs and what would make each option wrong. The largest is that
**§22 registers six steadiness verdicts and names no threshold for any of them**,
so `SPREAD_TOLERANCE` ships empty and the verdict refuses for every parameter;
the grading tree is written from §22's six descriptions and tested against
fictional figures, and starts producing verdicts the moment the owner supplies
two numbers per parameter. Four parameters have no analysis window, five no
per-day movement threshold, four no clearly-out margin, and five questions have
two canon answers apiece.
what is deliberately not done: nothing calls it, no classifier is deleted, no
surface changes, and the engines are untouched. **The golden fingerprint held at
`3a782222dbce41c5`**, which is the plan's own test that this stage did not reach
further than intended.
in plain terms: the app had sixteen different bits of code deciding whether your
alkalinity is high, low or fine, and none of them checked with the others. This
is the one piece of code that will answer that question from now on, written
only from the rules you have written down. Where your rules do not cover
something it says so out loud instead of guessing — and the twenty places it had
to say so are written up for you. Nothing on your screen changes yet.
