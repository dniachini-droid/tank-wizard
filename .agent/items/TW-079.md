why: `THE-ENGINE-PLAN-v2.md` Stage 6a. `.agent/engine-inventory.md` counted
**sixteen position classifiers** across ~76 decision sites with no referee, and

what it is: `src/lib/classify/classify-reading.js`, one exported function
answering four questions about one reading and nothing else —
  - its `wizard-states.md` §13 band, from canon's seven and no others;
  - its §22 steadiness verdict over the analysis window;
  - whether it is moving, per `reef-chemistry.md` §11 as amended 16 August —
    the fitted daily rate exceeds the element's threshold, **or** the direction
    has held and the total movement over the window clears §5's noise floor;
  - whether it is out and whether clearly out, per §26 and §27, from the last
    reading only and never a fitted value.
every threshold is Stage 5's: one noise-floor table (§5, eight parameters, all
absolute, ammonia deliberately absent), alert levels for ammonia at anything
detectable and salinity below 33 / above 36 (§18), the clearly-out margins of
§27 and §29.3, and §18's midpoint-hung offsets with §10's magnesium floor.
§18's own sentence naming this function — "classifyReading validates this on
every call and returns insufficient-data with a configuration error" — is
implemented and tested.
built from canon alone, per the plan's governing rule: nothing was read out of
`findings.js`, `narrative-engine.js`, `reading-meaning.js`, `stability-engine.js`,
`state.js` or the three engines. Where canon is silent the function **refuses and
names what is missing** rather than inheriting a figure — §13's last row and
§22's "Unknown refuses" are the mechanism canon already provides.
