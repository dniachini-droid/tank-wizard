why: reef-chemistry.md §28, decided 2026-08-15 and rewritten 2026-08-16. Moving a
level on purpose is a plan the user opts into, offered in both directions on the
same terms. Downward it executes as a deliberate under-dose (there is no additive
that lowers alkalinity, calcium or magnesium, so this is the only way down);
upward it executes as a correction. Today the app offers neither. A keeper at
455 ppm against a 400-450 band is told to decrease the dose to match consumption,
which parks calcium at 455 and calls it finished — the `off-target` card
(state.js:459 onward).
what the 16 Aug rewrite added, and it changes the shape of the work: §28.1 —
automatic dose advice only ever stabilises, so a recommended dose figure is always
the figure that matches consumption and never carries a move; §28.2 — a return
plan is offered only when the level is already stable and out of band, and a
dose-change suggestion and a return-plan offer may never share a card or a
surface (wizard-states.md §14 carries the same rule as a hard rule). Those two are
assertions about code that exists TODAY and can be tested before any of this is
built — see "tests it needs".
what §28 already settles, so it is not re-argued: destination is §9's middle-third
arrival zone; going down the rate is consumption's, not chosen; a duration estimate
is required because it IS the decision the user makes; §3's rails still cap the cut
or the addition; the return dose is recomputed not replayed; §9's two-reading
arrival test and calendar expiry both apply; the offer is one offer, not two dose
buttons.
what §28 leaves open, and must not be answered by an agent: new wizard state vs
correction plan with a signed delta (wizard-states.md §3 gains a row either way,
and which row depends on this); which wizard states satisfy "stable and out of
band" — 21c `off-target` plainly does, route 12 `worked` may (a Stage 4 gap-report
question, filed at §28.6 and at wizard-states.md §24.3); how the offer is presented
beyond the constraint above; what happens if the user does nothing.
CLOSED 16 Aug, no longer open: whether the upward case is built at all. It is,
symmetrically, on the same terms.
in plain terms: if your alkalinity climbs above your range there is nothing you can
pour to bring it down — you dose less than the tank uses and let it fall. The app
has never been able to offer that, so it tells you to cut the dose to the point the
level stops moving, which leaves you sitting above your range indefinitely. The
16 Aug decision says that advice is right and is deliberately all of the advice:
get steady first, then be offered the trip home as a separate choice.
spec: docs/spec/reef-chemistry.md §28, §1 (the three instruments and the
execution asymmetry), §9 (the machinery it reuses); docs/spec/wizard-states.md
§24.3 (the card that carries the offer), §14 (the exclusion as a hard rule)
repro: none — the feature does not exist. §28's "Enforced by" says so plainly
rather than implying a test.
tests it needs: all of them; none exist. Two are writable now and do not wait on
the design — no surface shows a dose-change suggestion and a return-plan offer
together (§28.2), and no recommended dose figure is anything but the figure that
matches consumption (§28.1).
