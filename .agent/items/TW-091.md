id: TW-091
title: the volume ceiling applies to one day's dose, not to the whole correction
status: needs-approval
tags: —
order: 1020

why: `wizard-states.md` §25.6 item 1, decided 16 August (Stage 5c).
`reef-chemistry.md` §9's wrong-tool rule was half-amended on 16 August — the app
must not quote an impossible volume (kept) and must not point at another product
(withdrawn, because the constraint is the rate and naming a faster product just
makes an unsafe change easier to perform). **That left the ~1.5 L ceiling looking
redundant**, since a correction spread over nine days brings the daily volume
right down and the ceiling would never fire.
the decision: **it survives, and what changes is what it is measured against.**

> If one day of a plan needs more than about 1.5 L of the maintenance solution,
> **the plan is too aggressive — however many days it runs.**

**A sanity check on a day's dose, not a trigger to reach for another product.**
The per-correction reading is withdrawn: a correction totalling 4 L over nine
days is a correction, not a wrong-tool case, and the arithmetic that called it
one was measuring the wrong thing.
**a day's dose over the ceiling means more days, not a different bottle.** §3's
rails apply however the alkalinity gets there. The ceiling and the rails now
point the same way, which is why the rule survives at all — it is a second check
catching a plan the rails alone would let through, expressed in the unit a keeper
actually pours.
what this must not reintroduce: any mention of dry buffer, a water change, or any
alternative product, on any surface, in any state. `wizard-states.md` §2's branch
18 and §3's "Wrong tool" tab label still exist and still fire; **the label names a
product judgement the app no longer makes** and is Stage 6c's rewrite, not this
item's.
read with: TW-087 (§24.23, the card that carries the gradual plan and the honest
duration), TW-079.
spec: docs/spec/reef-chemistry.md §9 (the rules list and "The volume ceiling
applies per day"), §3; docs/spec/wizard-states.md §24.23, §25.6 item 1
repro: the ceiling is applied to a correction's total volume today, so a
multi-day plan can be called a wrong-tool case on a total no single day
approaches.
tests it needs: a nine-day plan whose total exceeds 1.5 L and whose daily dose
does not, passing; a plan whose **daily** dose exceeds 1.5 L failing as too
aggressive and being restaged over more days rather than redirected to another
product; and no alternative-product string reachable from either path.
