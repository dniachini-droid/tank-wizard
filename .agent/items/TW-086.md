title: salinity becomes a graded parameter — window, rate thresholds, clearly-out margin, and its alert off the range edge
status: needs-approval
tags: —
order: 970

why: `.agent/stage-6a-gaps.md` W-1, W-2, W-4 and T-3. Salinity appeared in three
separate "canon grades nothing for this" lists while being **a parameter the app
already acts on** — `reef-chemistry.md` §18 gave it an alert tier on 16 August
and §3 has given it a rate rail since the canon swap. A parameter the app will
act on ought to be one it can grade.
the four figures, decided 16 August (Stage 6a):
  - **a 14-day analysis window** (`reef-chemistry.md` §4) — the same as
    alkalinity's, for a parameter that moves on the timescale of days. Without
    it `classifyReading` refuses with `no-analysis-window` and neither a movement
    claim nor a steadiness verdict is reachable.
  - **a moving threshold of 0.2 ppt/day** (§11's first limb) and a **`sliding`
    bar of 0.4 ppt/day**, which is not a second decision — `wizard-states.md`
    §22 sets `sliding` at twice the moving threshold for every parameter that has
    one, and 0.4 is that rule applied.
  - **a clearly-out margin of 0.5 ppt** (§27) — a fixed distance past the edge in
    salinity's own unit, declared as a bare number. It coincides in scale with
    §3's 0.5 ppt/day rail and **is not derived from it**; §27 rule 3 forbids
    defining a margin from another family of constant and nothing here does.
  - **its alert-high moves from 36 to 36.5 ppt** (§18), which is T-3 and is the
    one of the four that fixes a live wrong answer rather than filling a hole.
**T-3 in full, because it is the reachable bug.** §18 set the tier at "above 36
ppt" and the app's shipped salinity range is 34–36, so 36.0 was **both
`alert-high`** (§13's boundary rule is *at or above*) **and not out of range**
(§27 measures out against the keeper's own edges, and 36.0 is on the edge, not
past it). That combination is **a state no card in `wizard-states.md` §24
covers**, and it is reached by typing the most ordinary number a keeper with that
range can type. **The fix is the level, not the boundary rule** — §13 calls its
boundary rules "fixed, no exceptions" and exempting §18's two fixed levels would
make that sentence false for one figure. 36.5 clears the range edge and stays
inside `SAFE_BOUNDS`' 37. **The low side stays 33**; the shipped lower edge is 34
and no collision exists there.
potassium, pH and ammonia get **none** of the four, deliberately (§4, §11, §27,
§18). Do not file follow-ups for them. What a surface must do with a missing
clearly-out margin is stated in §27: **the parameter never escalates its
wording** — it says the level is out, plainly, however far out — and it does not
borrow another parameter's figure, does not scale one from the band, and does not
go quiet. `clearlyOut: null` must not reach a surface as a refusal.
salinity's **test cadence is deliberately not set** — §4's cadence column is
blank for it and the blank is carried at `.agent/needs-dan.md` item 15. Do not
invent one to fill the column. Nothing is blocked: the cadence feeds §30.2, which
is a bar about dose changes, and salinity has no dose.
read with: TW-085 (the steadiness figures, which give salinity 0.3/1.0 ppt and
depend on the rate threshold here), TW-030 (salinity, the parameter §25's
coverage table still says "nowhere" for), TW-079 (`classifyReading`, which
refuses for salinity on all four counts today).

repro: classify a salinity reading — `no-analysis-window`, then
`no-threshold-in-canon` on the rate limb, then `clearlyOut: null` when out. And
classify 36.0 against the shipped 34–36 range: `alert-high` with `out: false`.
tests it needs: 36.0 against the shipped range is in-band and not at the alert
tier, 36.6 is `alert-high`; a 14-day window graded for salinity end to end;
0.2 ppt/day exactly is moving and 0.4 exactly is `sliding`; 0.5 ppt past the edge
is clearly out and 0.4 past it is out but not clearly; and potassium, pH and
ammonia reaching **no** clearly-out escalation and **no** refusal for the want of
a margin.
