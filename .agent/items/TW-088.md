title: Tasks is the home for app-level notices — six of them, a count badge, and two that also render at the refusal
status: needs-approval
tags: —
order: 990

why: `.agent/gap-report.md` G-34 and `wizard-states.md` §25.6 item 3, decided
16 August (Stage 5c). Six notices are about the app rather than the tank — **no
net volume set, solution strength missing, strength unverified, testing sparsely,
kit replaced, and the kit comparison**. §25.1 evicted them from the tank summary,
which is one notice per parameter, and **they had nowhere to go**.
the rule, and it is the deliverable as much as the placement is: **the tank
summary tells you about your tank; Tasks tells you what to do about the app.**
Every future notice sorts into one or the other without a decision, which is the
point — this list will grow, and a rule that has to be re-argued per notice is
not a rule.
Tasks already holds reminders the keeper creates and now also holds what the app
notices, **in the same list**. The doubt recorded when this was deferred was
whether the two kinds fit; they do, because both are things to do and a keeper
does not care which of them thought of it. **The tab carries a count badge**,
which is not decoration: it is the one thing stopping this decision making the
app quieter than the summary was, by moving six notices behind a screen nobody
opens.
**two of the six also render inline, at the point of refusal.** No net volume set
(`reef-chemistry.md` §17) and solution strength missing (§16) do not merely want
doing — **they stop the app working**, and §12 refuses a dose without either. So
those two live in Tasks **and** show where the refusal happens, on the wizard and
every dosing surface that refuses for them (§12's three surfaces), so a keeper
looking at a blank dose figure sees why rather than going hunting. **The other
four appear in Tasks only.**
**the same wording in both places.** Bound by §16's rule that the missing input is
**named**, never a silent null or a NaN, and by §14. Two wordings for one missing
input is the second-wording fault §25.1's first rule exists to prevent.
this is **not** the summary's one-notice-per-parameter rule being bent: neither
of the two is about a parameter and neither is a verdict.
what is **not** decided and may not be picked by an implementer: **what a task
looks like** — how a notice becomes a task, whether it can be completed or only
resolved by fixing the thing, and how the app's entries sit beside the keeper's
in one list. The count badge implies they are counted together and says nothing
more. `wizard-states.md` §25.6 item 8, `.agent/needs-dan.md` item 15. **Tasks is
decided as the home, not as the design**, so this item delivers the routing and
the two inline refusals against whatever Tasks is when it is specified.
read with: TW-082 (the off switch — app-level notices are not parameters and the
per-parameter switch does not reach them), TW-081 (the notice ordering, which
governs the tank summary and not this list), TW-027.
spec: docs/spec/wizard-states.md §25.1, §25.4, §25.6 items 3 and 8;

repro: with no net volume set, the wizard produces no dose and the tank summary
carries no app-level notice — the reason is on neither screen.

headline; all six reachable in Tasks; the badge count matching the number of live
app-level notices plus the keeper's own; no net volume and no solution strength
each rendering at the refusal **and** in Tasks, in the same string; and the other
four rendering in Tasks only.
