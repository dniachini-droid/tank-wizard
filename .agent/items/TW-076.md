why: `wizard-states.md` §25.1 specified this layer in four words — *"the summary
headline only"* — for the single most-read string in the app.
`.agent/gap-report.md` G-27. `buildHeadline` (`narrative-engine.js:185-307`)
composes it from ~15 state clauses × ~12 qualifiers, *"well over a hundred
distinct lines"* by its own comment. It is also **what replaces the deleted
health score** (TW-068), so this is the item that decides whether the dashboard
has a tank-level view at all — **TW-068 must not ship before this one**, or the
collapsed summary falls back to whatever `buildHeadline` produces.
the rule, decided 16 August: **three slots, in order, each dropped when empty** —
the worst thing now, anything else notable, anything in flight.
worked example, all three filled: **"Alkalinity is out of range, and several
others are moving — a dose change is still settling."** With slot 3 empty it
stops at *"…and several others are moving."* With 2 and 3 empty it is
*"Alkalinity is out of range."* **A dropped slot leaves nothing behind** — no
placeholder clause, no "and nothing else".
naming: one, two or three out-of-range parameters are **named individually**; at
**four or more**, the **worst two are named** followed by **"several others"**.
The cut is on the count, never on screen width — a headline that reflows into a
different claim is two headlines.
worst is a two-key sort: **alert tier first** (§13 `alert-low`/`alert-high`;
`reef-chemistry.md` §18 for which parameters can reach them), **then furthest out
as a fraction of its own band** — distance past the nearer edge of the user's
target range, divided by that range's width. Both at stored precision, against
the last reading (§13, `reef-chemistry.md` §26).
why a fraction and not a distance: 0.3 dKH and 30 ppm are not two sizes of the
same thing. A raw distance sorts the tank by which parameter is measured in the
larger unit, and magnesium would lead every headline it appeared in.
**this is a ranking key, not a margin.** `reef-chemistry.md` §27 rule 3 forbids
defining a **margin** in terms of a band width and is untouched here: this key
gates no recommendation, suppresses none, relaxes no constraint and changes no
figure. `ALK_CLEARLY_OUT` and its two siblings keep their fixed values. Do not
let a reviewer conflate the two, and do not reuse this key for anything that
decides an action.
generated, never hand-composed: every clause comes from the same verdicts the
tiles render. **A literal in the summary's code path is a finding**, exactly as
it is for a card's short form. This is the property the score never had — the
keeper can check every clause by looking down the screen.
what fills each slot (§25.1's table, derived from canon and correctable in one
line): slot 1, the worst parameter whose §13 band is `alert-*` or
`out-of-band-*`; slot 2, the remaining out-of-range parameters under the naming
rule plus parameters that are **moving** (§11's rule and §11's word — not
`drifting`, not `unsettled`); slot 3, parameters whose wizard state is a change
or plan the keeper made that the app has not yet judged — `settling`, `due`,
`correcting`, `correcting-dose`, `correction-due`, `correction-stalled`.
`worked`, `fell-short`, `overshot` and `correction-done` are **judged** and are
not in flight; `suggested` has not been started and is not either.
still open, and an implementer may not pick: **what breaks an exact tie** — two
parameters, same tier, same fraction out. The order must be deterministic or the
headline cannot be checked against the tiles. The tiles' own order is the obvious
candidate and canon deliberately does not choose it.
`.agent/needs-dan.md` Open item 13 item 2, `wizard-states.md` §25.6 item 6.
not this item: **G-29**, what an "N of M in range" claim may count, is still

**note on the open tie-break above:** TW-081's fixed parameter order —
alkalinity, calcium, magnesium, salinity, nitrate, phosphate, potassium, ammonia
— is a total order over parameters and would break this tie cleanly. **It is
deliberately not adopted for the headline**, and an implementer may not adopt it.
`wizard-states.md` §25.6 item 6 records it as the candidate on the table.
