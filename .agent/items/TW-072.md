title: `TONE_TIER` redone in the four registered severity colours — and the direction/tier collision it exposes
status: needs-approval

why: `wizard-states.md` §15's colour registry, amended 2026-08-16 (Stage 5).
`TONE_TIER` (`src/lib/analytics/reading-meaning.js:121`) maps **six** hex
colours to three tiers to implement §22's "never renders calmer than the
reading". §15 registers **four** severity colours, and four of `TONE_TIER`'s six
— `#2A8050`, `#1D6FA5`, `#A2621B`, `#C4285B` — are registered nowhere. An
unregistered colour is the same fault as an unregistered word, and six severity
colours is more than anyone distinguishes at a glance.
the mapping to build, from §15: calm (`in-band`, `drifting`) → `ok` `#0B7C86`;
raised (`out-of-band-low`, `out-of-band-high`) → `low` `#926A09`; alert
(`alert-low`, `alert-high`) → `high` `#C4285B`; ungradeable
(`insufficient-data`) → `unknown` `#9FB0AE`. The tiers are §13's bands, not a
fifth severity scale, and the tier comes from the **latest** reading
(`reef-chemistry.md` §26).
**the part that is Dan's and must be settled before this is built.** The same
four colours already mean **direction** elsewhere: `paramStatus`
(`src/lib/dates.js:25-29`) returns `low` under the minimum and `high` over the
maximum, and `DosingWizard.jsx:69`, `DoseExpectation.jsx:221`,
`ReadingContext.jsx:86` and `backup.jsx:347` all colour by it. Under the mapping
above, `low` means *out of range either side* and `high` means *at the alert
tier either side* — the same two colours meaning one thing on a verdict and
another on a chip, which is the fault the registry exists to prevent, one level
down from words.
the three options, none chosen: `paramStatus`'s colouring moves to the tier
reading; or it keeps direction and stops using these four colours; or the
registry gains a name for the distinction. §15 records the mapping as decided
because §22 needs an escalation a direction-keyed mapping cannot give — a lethal
low and a mildly low rendering identically is the exact failure §22's tier was
added to stop. **Which way `paramStatus` goes is not decided.** Carried in
`.agent/needs-dan.md`.
what does not move either way: `STATUS_COLOR`'s four values, pinned by
`src/test/spec/terminology/colour-registry.test.js:69`, and the decided-against
alkalinity/`ok` collision (TW-046).
in plain terms: the app uses six colours to say how bad something is, and only
four of them are written down anywhere. It drops to the four. The catch is that
two of those four are also used to mean "too low" and "too high" on other
screens, so the same amber would mean two different things depending where you
look — and which of those two meanings wins is a question for you before anyone
writes the code.
spec: docs/spec/wizard-states.md §15's colour registry (the four-colour
mapping), §22's alert tier, §13; docs/spec/reef-chemistry.md §26
repro: `grep -n 'TONE_TIER' -A 8 src/lib/analytics/reading-meaning.js` — six
colours, four of them in no registry.
tests it needs: extend `colour-registry.test.js` so no severity colour outside
`STATUS_COLOR` is rendered anywhere; a tier table test; and the §22 case — an
alert-tier reading never renders in the calm colour.
