id: TW-071
title: One word for position — tiles read in range, above range, below range
status: needs-approval
tags: —
order: 820

why: `wizard-states.md` §15, registered 2026-08-16 (Stage 5). Found by Dan on
the dashboard on 16 August: the banner said **"Out of range"** and the tile
directly beneath it said **"ABOVE BAND"**. Same reading, same instant, two
vocabularies, one tap apart.
the registered words: **in range**, **above range**, **below range**. "Band" is
canon's own word — §13's seven bands, §2's edges — and stays canon's: the app
never says it out loud, the same category as the `off-target` state id and
"drift back".
the work: every user-facing string that says band, out of band, above band,
below band, in band, on target or off target for a **position** becomes one of
the three registered words. `wordingcheck.mjs` is where the ban belongs, so it
cannot come back — it already owns this class of check.
what must not be swept up in it: `classifyReading`'s band **ids** (`in-band`,
`out-of-band-low`, `alert-high` and the rest) are not copy and do not change.
This is a rename of what is rendered, not of the vocabulary the engine reasons
in — §13's table is unaffected and its seven names stay exactly as they are.
read with TW-035 and the target-terminology work: the same registry's 16 August
"target range" decision renamed a neighbouring set of strings, and the two
should not fight over the same lines twice.
in plain terms: your dashboard called the same alkalinity reading "out of range"
at the top of the screen and "ABOVE BAND" in the tile underneath. The app now
has one set of words for where a level sits — in range, above range, below
range — and "band" becomes an internal word it never says to you.
spec: docs/spec/wizard-states.md §15 (the four position rows and the range
decision), §13, §14
repro: the dashboard banner and the parameter tile beneath it, on any
out-of-range reading.
tests it needs: extend `scripts/verify/wordingcheck.mjs` with the banned
position synonyms; a test that the three registered phrases are the only
position words any surface renders.
owner: implementer, once [approved]
