why: `.agent/gap-report.md` G-2 — §2 branch 12, tab label "Steady, out of range",

the card: **"The change worked, but alkalinity is settled below your range /
8.0 dKH, holding steady since the change. Your dose is now matching what the tank
uses."** plus **"Plan a gradual return to 8.5 dKH →"**.
why the offer belongs here: this is `reef-chemistry.md` §28.2's
stable-and-out-of-band state, which is the only condition for a return plan and
is now satisfied by exactly two states — branch 21c (`off-target`, §24.3) and
this one. §28.3's reasoning applies unchanged: once the level is steady the dose
matches consumption and the dose figure has nothing left to say, it holds the
level where it is, and where it is, is wrong. **A fourth card acquiring the offer
is a finding** unless §28.2's condition moved with it — a fourth, because §24.23
already renders the phrase on a level far below range that is usually neither
stable nor still, and whether that is a correction wearing the return plan's
words is §25.6 item 2 and is not this item's to answer.
what the current code says, and why it is a finding: `state.js:319-320` renders
*"Alkalinity is steady but not where you want it"* + *"the level itself needs a
separate correction"* — which offers a **correction** in the state
`reef-chemistry.md` §28.2 names as the return plan's own. Replaced by §24.27's
wording, not patched.
§28.2's exclusion is satisfied, not strained: no dose-change suggestion appears
on this card because there is none to make — the dose matches consumption, which
is what *steady* means. The offer and a suggestion still never share a card, and
that is the assertion to write.
details that will look like mistakes and are not: the **tone stays grey** (§3),
because grey is *nothing to do about the dose* and the offer is not a dose —
§24.3 already pairs grey with the same offer. The **headline keeps the
parameter** (§23.7) because the sentence as a whole would not stand without it,
even though its first clause would. The **destination is the band midpoint**
(`reef-chemistry.md` §9), as on §24.3 and §24.23. **"Settled" here is ordinary
English and not §22's `steady-off` verdict** — same tank, different axis, and
neither may be rendered as the other.
