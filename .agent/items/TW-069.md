id: TW-069
title: Delete `paramContext` — nothing replaces it
status: needs-approval
tags: —
order: 800

why: `wizard-states.md` §25.2, decided 2026-08-16 (Stage 5). Eight prose blocks
(`src/lib/analytics/reading-meaning.js:17-87`) fire whenever the last reading is
outside the user's band and render on the history modal below the steadiness
verdict, quoting about fifteen figures canon has never named — against a band
the keeper set themselves.
the one that is not merely unnamed but wrong: calcium high tells a keeper *"tanks
run happily up to around 500-550 ppm"* (`:33`) where `reef-chemistry.md` §2 caps
calcium at **500 because above that it pulls alkalinity down**. The app
contradicts its own spec, on a screen, in prose.
the rest of the figures: magnesium *"1500s"* and *"the 1300-1400 most guides
quote"* against §2's 1275-1425 and the 1380 salt-mix trigger; phosphate 0.15,
which appears nowhere and which §29.2 specifically forbids treating as a fault;
nitrate *"dose back up to around 5 ppm"*; potassium 430 / 360 / 380-420 / 20,
for a parameter canon has no reasoning about at all.
it is also the largest concentration of wording-rule breaches in the app —
§23.5 (never speculate about causes) and `reef-chemistry.md` §29.6 (no levers):
*"water changes are the likely source"*, *"low magnesium is usually the reason
calcium won't hold"*, *"a little more feeding is normally the fix"*. See D-11.
**nothing replaces it, by decision.** The band says where the level is and the
verdict says how steady it has been. Explaining what that means is the kind of
writing that goes stale and then contradicts something else, which is exactly
what happened here. An implementer may not draft a shorter, safer version.
the two figures worth keeping are already canon and are read from there, not
from this block: 0.5 dKH/day is `reef-chemistry.md` §3's rail, and the 380 ppm
calcium floor is §2 layer 1.
in plain terms: the paragraphs under your history chart that explain what a
reading means are going. One of them tells you 550 calcium is fine when your own
spec stops at 500 because higher pulls your alkalinity down; several guess at
causes the app cannot see. Nothing takes their place — the app tells you where
the level is and how steady it has been, and stops there.
spec: docs/spec/wizard-states.md §25.2 (the deletion), §23.5;
docs/spec/reef-chemistry.md §2, §3, §29.2, §29.6
repro: log a calcium reading above your range and read the history modal — it
recommends a level canon forbids.
tests it needs: a test that no surface renders parameter prose outside the
registered card set (§24), which is the general form of this fault and also
catches the next hand-written block.
owner: implementer, once [approved]
