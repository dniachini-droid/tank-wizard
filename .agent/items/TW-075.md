why: `reef-chemistry.md` §24, decided 14 August, has required a marked idle card
echoing the wizard and an escalation at three consecutive negatives ever since.
**Neither existed in canon or in code** — `.agent/gap-report.md` G-3, the only
gap where a full four-part rule in canon had no wording anywhere to render it.
The wording is now `wizard-states.md` §24.24, written 16 August.
the card: **"Alkalinity is rising faster than your dose accounts for / 9.1 dKH,
up from 8.6 two days ago. The dose is unchanged. Test again in two days."**
the escalation, at three consecutive negatives with nothing logged:
**"Alkalinity has been rising faster than your dose accounts for since 10 August
/ Three readings running. The dose is unchanged."**
one card, two wordings: one state at two evidence levels. §24's action is `hold`
at every step and *escalating changes what is said, never what is dosed*. The
`state` stays `"idle"` and the hold is **marked**. **Do not add a branch** —
`wizard-states.md` §2's ordered list does not move for this, and a fourth `idle`
route in a first-match list would sit below two branches that reach the same
reading first.
what the card must not say, and this is the point of the item: **no cause, at
either level.** `reef-chemistry.md` §24 part 4's "wrong Setup strength or
collapsed demand" is **withdrawn** and §23.5 holds without exception. **No water
change, in either direction** — §22 stopped subtracting them from consumption at
every layer on 16 August, so the app has nothing to say about one. **No missing
one-off correction** — naming one as absent is naming a cause. The escalation
carries no retest instruction either; three retests two days apart have already
happened by the time it fires.
what the current code says, and why it is a finding: `state.js:499-503`
(`a.gainingHold`) renders *"Check whether a water change or a one-off correction
is missing from the log, then test again in two days."* **Both halves of that
sentence are now against canon.** The string is replaced by §24.24's, not
patched.
still open, and an implementer may not pick: whether the card gains a second
wording naming a **logged** correction. `reef-chemistry.md` §24 part 3's first
branch survives — naming something the keeper told the app is a fact, not a
guess — but the card written is the nothing-logged case, which is the one the
escalation counts. `.agent/needs-dan.md` Open item 13 item 1, and

in plain terms: when your tank gains alkalinity faster than your dose explains,
the app says exactly that, holds your dose, and asks you to retest in two days.

why, because the likeliest guess is that the bottle strength you entered is
wrong, and if you go and change a figure that was right, every dose it works out
for you afterwards is wrong too.
