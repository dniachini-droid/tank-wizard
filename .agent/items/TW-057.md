why: `reef-chemistry.md` §29.5, decided 16 Aug. **Three of the last four
readings outside the band** is what the app says about phosphate instead of a
direction, and nitrate gets the same count alongside its trend (TW-058).
Nothing in the app counts this today — every other summary fits a line — so
this is a **new mechanism, not a retuned one**.
what it says that a slope cannot, and why it is the honest summary for a
parameter that oscillates: *this is where you have been living*, and *this is
not one odd test*. Dan: *"Mine oscillates. Mine goes up to 0.20, then down to
0.15, then up to 0.19, then down. It doesn't work like that."*
**same side — settled 16 Aug, later the same day.** The item was filed blocked
on this and is now unblocked. Two above and one below **does not count**. Dan:
*"The point of the count is where the level has been living; a reading bouncing
above then below is not that."* Three high and one low is a tank living high;
two high, one low and one high is a tank bouncing, which is what phosphate does
when nothing is wrong — so the literal either-side reading would have fired
hardest on the exact behaviour this mechanism exists to stop the app talking
about. **Nothing in this item is open.**
scope:
  - the predicate is **three of the last four on the same side of the band** —
    three above, or three below. Mixed sides do not count, whatever the total
    outside.
  - the count is over the **last four readings**, not a window in days — it is
    a count, and §29.7's windows govern the stability layer, not this.
  - fewer than four readings: refuse and name what is missing, per §13's last
    row and the §22 refusal TW-037 built. Do not count three of three.
  - readings are tested against **the user's band** (§29.2), not layer 3.
  - the count is a statement, not a trigger: it changes no figure, per §29.6.
in plain terms: instead of drawing a line through your phosphate tests and
claiming it is heading somewhere, the app counts. Three of your last four
outside your range, **all on the same side**, is worth telling you about. Three
high and one low means you are sitting high. Two high, one low and one high is
just phosphate bouncing, and the app stays quiet — which is the whole reason
the count exists instead of a trend line. One odd test never counts.
spec: docs/spec/reef-chemistry.md §29.5; §29.2 for whose band; §13 for the
refusal when evidence is thin
repro: none — the mechanism does not exist. Its first artefact is the test that
defines it. The case that must be written first is the negative one: readings
above, above, below, above must produce **nothing**.
