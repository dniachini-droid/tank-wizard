what it says that a slope cannot, and why it is the honest summary for a
parameter that oscillates: *this is where you have been living*, and *this is
not one odd test*. Dan: *"Mine oscillates. Mine goes up to 0.20, then down to
0.15, then up to 0.19, then down. It doesn't work like that."*
**BLOCKED on one line from Dan, and it is named in §29.5.** *"Three of the last
four readings outside the band"* reads literally as **either side**, while its
stated purpose (*this is where you have been living*) reads as **one side**.
The separating case is two above and one below in the last four. An
implementer needs the answer before writing the predicate; guessing it is
exactly the kind of invention §25 forbids. Everything else in this item is
settled.
scope once that is answered:
  - the count is over the **last four readings**, not a window in days — it is
    a count, and §29.7's windows govern the stability layer, not this.
  - fewer than four readings: refuse and name what is missing, per §13's last
    row and the §22 refusal TW-037 built. Do not count three of three.
  - readings are tested against **the user's band** (§29.2), not layer 3.
  - the count is a statement, not a trigger: it changes no figure, per §29.6.
in plain terms: instead of drawing a line through your phosphate tests and
claiming it is heading somewhere, the app counts. Three of your last four
outside your range is worth telling you about; one odd test is not. The one
thing still to settle is whether three *outside* means three on the same side —
two high and one low in the last four is the case that decides it, and that is
a line from you.
