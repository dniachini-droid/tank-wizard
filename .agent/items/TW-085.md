    | parameter  | tight (at or under) | wide (at or over) |
    | alkalinity | 0.3 dKH             | 1.0 dKH           |
    | calcium    | 25 ppm              | 80 ppm            |
    | magnesium  | 50 ppm              | 100 ppm           |
    | salinity   | 0.3 ppt             | 1.0 ppt           |

**four parameters, not nine.** phosphate, nitrate, potassium, pH and ammonia get
**no steadiness verdict at all** — a decision, not a missing row. They must not
be graded against another parameter's spread, must not have one scaled from their
band, and **must not produce a refusal message either**: the surface shows no
steadiness word for them and says nothing about it. A permanent *"steadiness
cannot be graded for potassium"* is the line-the-keeper-stops-reading that §32.3
refuses for ammonia, and worse, because it implies a figure is coming.
the four graded are exactly the four with a §11 rate threshold, and that is
structural rather than coincidental: `sliding` is defined off the rate, so a
parameter without one cannot reach all six verdicts, and six words on some
parameters and four on others is §13's silent two-band vocabulary in a new place.
**the figures are decided, not derived.** Not multiples of §5's noise floor —
that would make changing a test kit change what counts as steady, which is the
fault §27 rule 3 names — and not fractions of the band. Do not re-derive them.
three rules make them usable, all in §22:
  - **spread is over the full window** — highest minus lowest, every reading in
    the window being graded (§25.2's selected window where the panel supplies
    one, `reef-chemistry.md` §4's otherwise). Not a standard deviation, not an
    IQR, not a recent slice.
  - **the off-range test is the median of the most recent half of the window.**
    Three verdicts turn on whether the window sits off the range and §22 forbids
    deciding it from a single reading. Median because a kit misread is the
    commonest outlier and a mean chases it; recent half because a tank that spent
    the first fortnight out and the last fortnight in **is in**.
  - **`sliding` is twice §11's moving threshold** — 0.20 dKH/day, 10 ppm/week,
    20 ppm/week, 0.4 ppt/day. One decision, four consequences, **no fifth
    constant**; graded off the fitted rate and never off the spread figures.
the grading tree is **derived and correctable in one line**, and is written into
§22 so two surfaces cannot derive it differently: `sliding` first (rate at or
over twice §11's); then `loose` (spread at or over wide, direction not held);
then the off-range test picks the pair and the spread picks within it —
in-band/at-or-under-tight `dialled`, in-band/over `controlled`, off-band/at-or-
under-tight `steady-off`, off-band/over `unsettled`. **Do not treat the tree as
decided figures**; do not add a seventh verdict.
what still refuses: a **graded** parameter whose window cannot be graded this
time — too few readings, or no computable window — refuses and names what is
missing, per §22's "Unknown refuses". That is a different state from the five
with no verdict and must not render as the same string.
