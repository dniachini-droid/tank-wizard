title: Kit accuracy — delete alkalinity's outright, strip the verdict from calcium's and magnesium's
status: needs-approval
tags: —
order: 940

why: `.agent/gap-report.md` D-1, the one deletion in that report worked up with
three options rather than taken as written. `kit-<param>`
(`findings.js:191-211`): *"Your alkalinity kit read 25% higher than the lab
across 3 paired comparisons … worth replacing the reagent before acting on it."*
**`reef-chemistry.md` §19 and `wizard-states.md` §14 both say the app never
tells a user their kit is wrong**, and the argument for an exception was that
the rule's stated reasoning is about **drift**, which an independent lab
measurement is not.
the decision, 16 August (Stage 5b): **answered in two parts, differently.**
**alkalinity: `kit-alkalinity` is deleted outright, and not for the rule's
reason.** **ICP does not measure carbonate alkalinity.** An ICP panel measures
elements; alkalinity is a titration result — the water's capacity to neutralise
acid. The two numbers are not measurements of the same quantity, so the
percentage between them is not an error, a bias or a calibration offset. **This
is a stronger deletion than the rule gives**: no future decision about ICP
reopens it, because there is no comparison to permit.
**calcium and magnesium: the observation survives, the verdict does not.** ICP
does measure both, as elements, against the same water. The finding becomes the
statement about the **comparison** and stops:
> *"Your last three calcium readings sat 25% above the lab panel taken the same
> day."*
and **nothing after it** — no *"your kit is reading high"*, no *"worth replacing
the reagent"*, no *"before acting on it"*.
**the kit is not named as wrong because the app does not know which of the two
is wrong.** A lab is a better measurement, not a true one, and the sample was
drawn, stored and posted by the keeper. This is §23.5's shape applied to a number
instead of a cause: report what the record contains, stop before the
explanation.
**nothing downstream moves.** The keeper's readings remain what every band,
verdict, trend and dose is computed from. This finding corrects nothing,
suppresses nothing, gates nothing.
the two live figures split, and neither is redesigned: **5% is ratified as the
trigger** on §31's terms (below it the finding does not fire; a finding that
fires on every paired difference is noise). **25% goes with the verdict it
graded** — a severity ladder is the app ranking how wrong the kit is, which is
the claim being removed. **One wording, one severity, at `watch`.** `act` would
contradict a notice that asks for nothing.
**no new figure may be minted.** The pairing window at the call site —
`computeCalibration(readings, icps, paramDefs, 7, kitChanges)` — is untouched and
uncanonised, which is the state it was already in.
placement is unchanged and unsettled: `kit-*` is one of the six ids G-34 has no
home for, and `wizard-states.md` §25.6 item 3 owns that question. **This item
does not place it.**
not this item: `kit-replaced-<param>`, which is a different finding and is in the
same G-34 list.
spec: docs/spec/reef-chemistry.md §19 (the lab-paired comparison), §31;
docs/spec/wizard-states.md §14, §23.5, §25.6 item 3
repro: a tank with ICP panels logged alongside its own readings and a mean
offset over 5%.
tests it needs: `kit-alkalinity` unreachable for any input; calcium's and
magnesium's firing at ≥5% with the comparison sentence and no verdict clause; no
severity above `watch`; the phrases "your kit", "reagent" and "before acting on
it" absent; no computed figure anywhere altered by the presence of ICP data.
