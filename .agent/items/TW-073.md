title: Withdraw the retarget offer — §28 wins the contradiction with §18
status: needs-approval
tags: —
order: 840

why: `reef-chemistry.md` §18, amended 2026-08-16 (Stage 5). §18 said that where
a value sits outside the user's target range but the series is stable, the app
**suggests reconsidering the range** before suggesting a correction. §28.2,
written two days later, gives that exact state — stable and out of band — as the
one occasion the app offers to **walk the level home**. Two sections, one state,
opposite answers. **§28 wins; §18's clause is withdrawn.**
the reasoning recorded at §18: offering to move the goalposts is a strange
default. The range was set for a reason, and a range that moves to meet the
reading cannot then be evidence about the reading.
what goes with the clause: `computeControl`'s `suggestWorth`
(`src/lib/analytics/reading-meaning.js:290`, `suggestDiff > step × 1.5`), the
retarget offer it feeds, and `SnoozeSheet`'s *"the target range is the thing to
change rather than the dose"*. Dan saw the live version on the dashboard:
*"Change the target range to 8.7-9.3? Use this."*
what stays: the sentence above the withdrawn clause — stability at a slightly
sub-optimal number beats movement toward an optimal one — which is §28.1 and
§28.2 in one line. And the user's own ability to edit their range in Setup at
any time; §2 is untouched. **What the app may no longer do is propose it.**
sequencing against TW-048: this item removes the wrong offer; TW-048 builds the
right one (the return plan). They are independent — the withdrawal does not
wait on the plan being built — but a keeper at 455 ppm with neither is told to
cut the dose and left at 455, which is §27's silence problem. Worth doing in
that order and saying so in the PR.
in plain terms: when a level sits outside your range but is holding steady, the
app has been offering to change your range to fit the reading. It stops. Your
range was set for a reason and the app is not going to talk you out of it — what
it should offer instead is a plan to walk the level back, which is a separate
piece of work.
spec: docs/spec/reef-chemistry.md §18 (the withdrawal), §28.1, §28.2, §2;
docs/spec/wizard-states.md §14's hard rule, §24.3
repro: a stable reading outside the band on the dashboard — *"Change the target
range to 8.7-9.3? Use this."*
tests it needs: no surface offers a target-range change; the §14 hard rule that
a dose-change suggestion and a return-plan offer never share a surface, which is
already writable against the app as it stands (TW-048).
