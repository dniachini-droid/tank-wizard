why: `THE-ENGINE-PLAN-v2.md` Stage 6c, and `wizard-states.md` §24, which says in
terms what this item is: *"These are not illustrations. They are the reference
wording, and Stage 6c builds the message layer to produce them. Where a card here
and a current app string differ, the card wins and the string is a finding."*
what it is: `src/lib/messages/message-layer.js`, one registry of §24's
twenty-seven cards plus §25's surface rules. `renderCard(id, facts)` returns a
card's headline, support, action and offer with the figures substituted;
`summaryLine` is §25.1's short form; `confirmationFor` is §25.3's deferral;
`collapsedHeadline` is §25.1's three slots.
**the rule it is built on: it does not invent wording.** Every string is §24's
with figures substituted. §24's preamble authorises exactly two substitutions —
**the parameter** (*"the same shapes apply to calcium and magnesium in their own
units"*) and, on **24.13 alone**, direction (*"a correction that pushed upward
risks continuing upward, so it says rising"*). Everywhere else the layer
**refuses the mirror** with `no-canon-wording-for-direction`, naming the card and
the direction, rather than composing the opposite sentence. A sentence written
here would be a second reference wording minted by an implementer, which is what
§25.1's first rule and §11's single-source rule both exist to prevent.
**the wizard owns the verdict.** Nothing here classifies, grades, compares
against a band or decides which card applies — the card id arrives as an
argument, from `classifyReading` (6a) and `doseStatus` (6d). Where a surface
appears to need a claim the wizard cannot supply, it is reported as a gap in the
wizard and **not** computed locally.
refusals are §14's shape, the same as `classifyReading`'s: a missing figure names
itself (`missing-figures: ratePerDay, readingCount, dose, matchingDose`) rather
than rendering a sentence with a hole in it, and a card with no parameter refuses
because §23.7 assumes the badge.
the wording rules are asserted, not assumed: §23.7's fragment-versus-sentence
split over both of §24's own lists, §23.4's units, §23.3's three dose cases
including the third one it calls easy to drop, §23.2's permitted second person,
§23.5 on 24.11 and 24.24 (no cause named, asserted by pattern), and §15's
registered **return plan** phrase appearing on **exactly** 24.3 and 24.27 with
24.23 named separately as the known exception — §24.3: *"a fourth card acquiring
it is a finding."*
**§25.1's eleven worked short forms are reproduced from the cards**, which is the
test that the generate-don't-write rule is actually generative: canon tabulates
them "as the check that the rule generates sensible lines rather than as a second
source", and if that is true the table is derivable. It is.
**the golden fingerprint held at `3a782222dbce41c5`.** Nothing calls the module,
no surface changes, `narrative-engine.js` and `buildFindings` are untouched.
the gaps that produced: **nine**, in `.agent/stage-6c-gaps.md`. **Five are
missing card wordings** — §24 words a situation in one direction and the tank
reaches the other: in range but rising (24.2's mirror), steady and **above** the
range (24.3's mirror, whose situation `reef-chemistry.md` §28.3 already works
through at 455 ppm), above-and-recovering and above-and-worsening (24.17/24.18's
mirrors), and `fell-short`/`overshot` in their unwritten quadrants. **These are
wording work for the owner**, of exactly the kind §24 itself is.
**one composition had to be chosen and is flagged**: §25.1's collapsed headline
reads as two clauses by its slot table and as one list by its naming rule, and
the two-clause reading produces *"Alkalinity is out of range, and calcium is out
of range"* at two parameters. **The one-list reading is taken**, because it
reproduces canon's only worked example exactly; one sentence in §25.1 settles it
(C-1).
**and one is a rule left with no checker**: after Stage 5c, placement is the
entire mechanism keeping 24.9 and 24.23 apart, and the regex that used to do it
is deleted. The layer carries `surface` on both cards and **cannot enforce the
rule** — only a caller can. **TW-087 should carry that check**, or §10's "a rule
with no checker is an intention" applies to it (A-2).
