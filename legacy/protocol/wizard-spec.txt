HANDOVER NOTES  -  read before giving this to anyone
================================================================================

Read out of the running code on 2026-08-12. Every state, guard and label was
verified against the build, not written from memory.

Companion document: dosing-spec.txt covers the ARITHMETIC - bands, rate limits,
how maintenanceDose is derived. This one covers the STATE MACHINE - what the
wizard says and in what order it decides. Read that one first if you want to
know what number the app produces; this one if you want to know why it is
showing a particular card.

Four things to watch:

1. ORDER IS THE DESIGN.
   The wizard is one ordered list of guards, first match wins. Moving a branch
   changes behaviour even when every condition is untouched. There is a real
   bug in this document's history: "correcting" sat below the dose verdicts,
   so a correction was detected and never reported. Lifting it fixed nothing
   else and fixed that entirely.

2. FOUR STATES ARE UNREACHABLE WITHOUT A STAGED PLAN.
   worked, fell-short, overshot and due live behind `plan`, which is a staged
   dose plan and NOT the same thing as a correction plan. 4,000 randomly
   generated tanks never reached them. Any agent testing the wizard must
   supply a staged plan or a quarter of the vocabulary goes unexercised.

3. THE WIZARD OWNS THE VERDICT. OTHER SURFACES ECHO IT.
   The summary and the reading confirmation must not form their own opinion.
   Two bugs came from that: a summary claim saying "parked off-target" against
   an engine saying "on its way to 475", and a confirmation saying "nothing to
   do" against a wizard asking for a dose change. If a surface disagrees with
   the wizard, the surface is wrong.

4. MAGNESIUM NEVER REACHES THE DOSE-GAP STATES.
   By design - see dosing-spec.txt section 5. It is managed by level. An agent
   who sees this as an inconsistency and "fixes" it will break it.

================================================================================
THE DOSING WIZARD  -  HOW IT DECIDES WHAT TO SAY
================================================================================

Alkalinity, calcium and magnesium. One function, doseStatus, 452 lines, called
once per element on every render.


1. What it is
-------------

The wizard turns an assessment - a set of numbers about one element - into a
single card the keeper reads. The card carries:

  state       one of 15, listed below
  tone        a colour, from a fixed set
  short       two or three words for the tab strip
  headline    one sentence
  detail      one or two sentences of reasoning
  target      where a plan is heading, when there is one
  testOn      when to test next, when that is the ask

Nothing else in the app is allowed to decide what an element's situation IS.
Other surfaces render this verdict or stay quiet.


2. The order of decision
------------------------

One ordered list. First match wins. Read top to bottom; a branch only runs if
every branch above it declined.

  #   STATE               FIRES WHEN
  --  ------------------  --------------------------------------------------
   1  blocked             the arithmetic is impossible - action is
                          "implausible", usually a wrong solution strength
   2  emergency           the level is outside SAFE_BOUNDS and no correction
                          plan is running
   3  correction-stalled  a plan has overrun its estimate and has not arrived
   4  correction-due      a plan is due to be checked and has not overrun
   5  correction-done     a plan has arrived or passed its target
   6  correction-stalled  a plan is going backwards, or has not moved at all
   7  correcting-dose     a plan is running normally
   8  correcting          a LOGGED one-off correction is still working through
   9  settling            a staged plan changed the dose and the settle window
                          has not passed
  10  due                 a staged plan is running and no test has been logged
  11  worked              tested, steady, and in band
  12  worked              tested and steady but not in band - "steady, off
                          target"
  13  fell-short          tested, and the engine still wants more in the same
                          direction
  14  overshot            tested, and the engine now wants the opposite
                          direction
  15  settling            a plain dose change inside its settle window
  16  suggested           the engine wants a different dose
  17  correcting          a correction is in progress by another route
  18  suggested           a one-off would need more than 25x the normal dose -
                          "wrong tool", pointing at dry salt or water changes
  19  suggested           a correction is needed rather than a dose change
  20  settling            the assessment is not usable yet - too few readings,
                          too little time
  21  off-target          the dose is right and the level is not
  22  idle                the dose is right, the level is slightly off
  23  idle                nothing to do

Order matters more than any individual guard. Three of the states appear more
than once
because the same conclusion is reached by different routes: correction-stalled
twice, settling three times, suggested three times, correcting twice, worked
twice and idle twice - 23 returns across 15 distinct states.


3. The states in full
---------------------

  STATE               TONE     TAB LABEL              MEANING
  ------------------  -------  ---------------------  --------------------------
  blocked             red      Check setup            the figures cannot be
                                                      trusted
  emergency           red      (danger wording)       outside safe bounds
  correction-stalled  amber    Taking too long        plan overran
  correction-stalled  amber    Not responding         plan is not working
  correction-due      amber    Test now               plan needs a reading
  correction-done     teal     Target reached         plan arrived
  correcting-dose     blue     Correction running     plan in progress
  correcting          blue     Correction running     logged correction in
                                                      progress
  settling            blue     Change settling        too soon to judge
  settling            blue     One more reading       not enough data yet
  due                 amber    Test to confirm        staged plan needs a test
  worked              teal     Change worked          it did what it should
  worked              grey     Steady, off target     stable in the wrong place
  fell-short          amber    Needs more             the change was too small
  overshot            amber    Went too far           the change was too large
  suggested           grey     Correction needed      a dose will not fix this
  suggested           grey     Wrong tool             the solution cannot do it
  idle                grey     Dose right, level off  holding, in the wrong
                                                      place
  idle                grey     No change needed       everything is fine

Tone is not decoration. Red means act now, amber means act soon, blue means
something is already in motion, teal means it worked, grey means nothing to do.


4. Corrections, in detail
-------------------------

Branches 3 to 8 all concern a correction. The flags come from
correctionProgress, computed from the plan and the readings since it started.

  arrived     the level has reached the target
  passed      the level has gone past the target
  dueNow      enough time has elapsed that a reading is expected
  overrun     days > (expected x 2) + 2
  stalled     3+ days in, at least one reading since, and the level has moved
              less than the kit noise floor
  backwards   3+ days in, at least one reading since, and the level has moved
              AWAY from the target

stalled and backwards both require a reading SINCE the plan started. Without
that condition the app would judge a correction on the reading that prompted
it, which is the stale-reading fault described in dosing-spec.txt section 7.

overrun is measured on the CALENDAR, not on readings. A plan left unattended
with no new tests still expires. Before this was added, a three-day plan with
no readings still said "on its way to 9.0dKH" at day 40.


5. What the wizard needs before it will suggest a dose
------------------------------------------------------

All four, from dosing-spec.txt section 5:

  at least 3 readings in the analysis window
  the settle window has passed since the last change
  the movement clears the kit's noise floor
  the gap is worth acting on

If any fails, the wizard says so rather than guessing: "settling" when it is
too soon, "one more reading" when there is too little data, "test to confirm"
when a change is waiting on evidence.

This is deliberate. An app that always has an answer is not more useful than
one that says what it is waiting for.


6. The distinction the whole wizard turns on
--------------------------------------------

A daily dose HOLDS a level. A correction MOVES one.

Three states exist purely to express this:

  off-target      the dose is right and the level is not. Nothing you add to
                  the daily dose fixes this - it would hold the level in the
                  wrong place faster. What is needed is a correction.

  idle "dose      the same conclusion when the level is only slightly out and
  right, level    the app is not pressing for action.
  off"

  suggested       the level needs to move and a dose change is the wrong
  "correction     instrument for it.
  needed"

If an agent collapses these into "increase the dose", the app starts giving
advice that cannot work.


7. Where the wizard must not be contradicted
--------------------------------------------

Three other surfaces speak about the same element:

  the tank summary claim
  the reading confirmation window
  the findings list

All three must ECHO the wizard or say nothing. Specifically:

  - the summary renders the wizard's headline and the first sentence of its
    detail. It does not write its own sentence.
  - the reading confirmation may use its own voice for the LEVEL, but must not
    give an instruction that conflicts. "Dead centre" is fine; "nothing to do"
    is not, when the wizard is asking for a change.
  - a correction running always outranks a dose-change message.

Enforced by tests/summary.js, tests/matrix.js and tests/sim/surfaces.js. A
contradiction fails the build.


8. Known faults
---------------

8.1  A slow decline is under-served  (UNRESOLVED)

     band === "stable" stays true for a fall of about 0.02 dKH a day, so a
     slowly crashing tank enters the hold branch and only the dose-gap trigger
     rescues it. Simulated over three years: three dose changes, alkalinity
     reached 0.5 dKH, and the wizard said "hold" at 6.87 - below the safe
     floor.

     The real fix is in stability GRADING, not in this state machine: a level
     outside its band and moving further out should never be graded stable,
     whatever the rate. See dosing-spec.txt section 9.1.

8.2  Four states need a staged plan to reach

     worked, fell-short, overshot and due. Not a fault in the app, a fault in
     how it has been tested - no sweep in the repo supplied a staged plan
     until tests/matrix.js. Any new test of the wizard must.
